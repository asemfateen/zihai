## 2026-07-06 - Accessible Password Toggles\n**Learning:** Password visibility toggle buttons (eye icons) often lack ARIA labels and keyboard accessibility (e.g., using `tabIndex={-1}`), making them unusable for screen reader and keyboard-only users.\n**Action:** When auditing forms, ensure all icon-only buttons have dynamic `aria-label`s reflecting their state and use `focus-visible` styles for clear keyboard focus indicators.
