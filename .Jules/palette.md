## 2024-05-17 - Missing ARIA labels on Icon-only Navigation Buttons
**Learning:** Icon-only buttons used in primary navigation (like Theme toggle, Profile, Settings, Logout) often lack `aria-label` attributes, relying solely on visual icons or generic `title` attributes for meaning. This creates a significant barrier for screen reader users who cannot interpret the icon visually.
**Action:** Always ensure that icon-only interactive elements (like `<button>` or `<a>`) have an explicit, descriptive `aria-label`. If the icon is decorative or its meaning is fully covered by the `aria-label`, ensure the icon itself (or inner elements like `<span>⚙️</span>`) has `aria-hidden="true"` to prevent redundant or confusing screen reader announcements. For toggle buttons like hamburger menus, additionally use `aria-expanded` to communicate the current state.
